Meet Your Specialist

Dr. Ashokumar Krishnamoorthi, MDS

Specialist in Prosthodontics & Implantology.

With advanced training in Prosthodontics, Dr. Ashokumar focuses on titanium root implants, complete dentures, implant restorations and full-mouth rehabilitation for patients who need more than a routine filling or single-tooth fix.

A prosthodontist considers the architecture of the entire smile: the missing root, the bite, the final crown, facial balance and long-term comfort.

Advanced Technology, Predictable Results

AI-assisted diagnostics and Digital Smile Design help map oral anatomy, bite forces and the final smile before treatment begins, supporting more predictable implant and prosthetic planning.

Clinical Philosophy: Precision with Compassion

“My goal is never just to fix a tooth. It is to restore confidence, comfort and quality of life through dentistry that is precise, honest and compassionate.”

Why See a Prosthodontist?

  • Complex care and full-mouth rehabilitation
  • Implant planning and restorations
  • Aesthetic smile design with functional bite planning
  • Comfort-focused denture and prosthetic solutions

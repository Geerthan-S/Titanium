Titanium Root Implants

Replacing the Root, Not Just the Tooth.

Tooth loss affects more than the visible smile. Once the natural root is missing, the jawbone loses the stimulation that helps maintain strength and shape.

A dental implant places a biocompatible titanium root into the bone. After healing, the implant supports a custom crown, bridge or full-mouth prosthesis planned for bite, comfort and natural appearance.

Biocompatible Foundation

Titanium is accepted by the body and can bond with bone through osseointegration, creating a stable base for replacement teeth.

Bone Preservation

Implants help transfer chewing forces into the jawbone, reducing the bone loss that often follows missing teeth.

Digital Smile Design

AI-assisted diagnostics and smile design planning help visualise the final result before the restoration is completed.

    Implant Questions

    Titanium Root Implant FAQ

    Does placing a titanium root hurt?

    The procedure is planned with local anaesthesia and comfort measures. Most patients describe pressure rather than pain during placement.

    What is the success rate?

    Dental implants commonly report success rates above 95% when case selection, placement, hygiene and maintenance are appropriate.

    Why choose a prosthodontist?

    A prosthodontist plans both the implant foundation and the final tooth replacement, balancing function, aesthetics and long-term comfort.

    Will I get a temporary tooth?

    Temporary options depend on the case. The consultation includes guidance on how your smile and chewing function can be managed during healing.

    Will the result look natural?

    Digital Smile Design helps plan tooth shape, size and position so the final restoration blends with your smile and bite.

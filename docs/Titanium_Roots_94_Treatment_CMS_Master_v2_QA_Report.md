## Major Changes Applied

1. Replaced all 94 short descriptions with unique treatment-card copy.
2. Rebuilt all Expected Outcome sections with grammatically correct, treatment-specific wording.
3. Replaced all 752 FAQs with treatment- or procedure-family-specific search-intent questions; no complete eight-question set is duplicated.
4. Rebuilt secondary keyword clusters using natural clinical terms, patient terminology, cost, recovery, suitability, question and local-intent variants.
5. Removed malformed automatic plurals and replaced unnatural pluralisation with a natural phrase or an explicit “not normally pluralised” instruction.
6. Replaced repeated recovery, contraindication, risk, cost and consultation boilerplate with treatment-specific or specialty-specific content.
7. Corrected all structured-data fallback wording.
8. Replaced grammatically unsafe “What Is [plural treatment]?” headings with natural heading structures.
9. Rebuilt the clinical source matrix using 67 distinct direct URLs across 376 reference entries, with more procedure-level sources from authoritative organisations.
10. Preserved draft status, noindex controls, unverified-service holds, pricing safeguards and verified-UUID restrictions.

## SEO Limitation

The revised keyword architecture is designed to strengthen topical relevance, patient-intent coverage, internal-link clarity and cannibalisation control. It does not guarantee rankings. Exact search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC and live conversion data were unavailable. Validate and refine the clusters after launch using Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, actual clinic-location data and page-performance evidence.

## Publication Holds

Do not publish until the clinic confirms its city, locality, address, phone, booking URL, treatments, technologies, materials, clinicians, credentials, pricing and referral pathways. Every record still requires review and sign-off by a registered dental practitioner.

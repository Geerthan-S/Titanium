Patient Experiences Are Shared Only with Permission.

Testimonials, photographs, videos, and treatment details are never published automatically. Patients can choose what information may be displayed and may request that approved content be updated or removed.

  • Consent before publication
  • Patient-controlled display name

  • Testimonial withdrawal support
  • Clinical-information minimisation

Testimonial FAQs

Are all testimonials genuine?

Yes. Testimonials are displayed only after verification, moderation, and patient consent.

Can I submit feedback without publishing it?

Yes. Patients may provide private feedback without granting permission for public display.

Can I withdraw a testimonial later?

Yes. Patients may contact the clinic to request that approved testimonial content be updated or removed.
